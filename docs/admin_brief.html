Admin brief for Spinach

What Spinach develops for the admin side, what HoA retains in Zoho and the consoles, and the contract between the two. Generated from the board, so it matches the admin screens, the Split page and the Admin and CRM tab. Picks confirmed 5 Oct 2026.

Cause: Vatsal, 5 Oct 2026 (W12: the pack for Spinach, after the Split page picks were confirmed)

1. Read me

Three admin screens are built: M03 the staff view of a client, M07 the health page, M10 the config screen. The logic panel L00 to L09 is built from the wireframes. Everything else on Spinach's July sheet and August sketches is read in Zoho or a console, fed by the app.

The rules behind every row: one way, app to Zoho (Zoho never writes into the app; the app may pull when it opens something, as H07 pulls the Desk thread); writes into the app by staff stay in the app; bands only in Zoho, no PAN, no financial numbers; the journey state is computed in the app and mirrored, Zoho never computes a state.

IDs: M screens are admin screens (the admin wireframes page), L screens the logic panel and A to X the app (the wireframes v0.2 tab), S1 to S25 the returning-user states (N01), I-numbers the integrations tab, T1 to T7 the annex tables at the end, G-numbers the gaps register, BE and FE the questionnaire answers of 24 Sep 2026.
