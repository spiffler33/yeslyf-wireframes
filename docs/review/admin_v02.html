  • O03's chosen time replaces the first scheduled nudge when it exists. Vatsal, 10 Sep 2026.

During the call the adviser enters inputs and the engine re-runs; nobody edits outputs by hand (spec v0.1, kept). brief V2.

  • State is computed on every open from stored flags; deep links land on the primary action (spec v0.1, kept).
