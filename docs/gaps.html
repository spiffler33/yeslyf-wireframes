G02 SEBI advertisement code review of reveal, sample plan, testimonials, listing, nudgesimpact Highsuggested owner: Harish
Recorded in the meeting: agreed, date set
The reveal copy, the sample plan, testimonials (X01 strip, day-7 nudge), the app store listing and the nudges are promotional communications by an RIA. The 2023 advertisement code restricts what can be said and requires prior approval of advertisements by the supervisory body; testimonials are likely restricted.
Vatsal recommendation: Compliance review of every promotional surface before Spinach writes copy; drop testimonials until cleared. Confidence on the exact testimonial rule: moderate; Harish to check.
G03 DPDP consent notice and deletionimpact Medsuggested owner: Harish
Recorded in the meeting: agreed, date set
A02 collects personal data and financial data follows. India's data protection law requires a consent notice with purpose, the right to withdraw, and deletion, with exceptions for records SEBI requires you to keep.
Vatsal recommendation: Draft the notice with counsel; A02 and H09 already have the slots; retention exceptions are listed in the admin/CRM spec.
