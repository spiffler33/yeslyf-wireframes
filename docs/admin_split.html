The rules the picks rest on

  • One way, app to Zoho: Zoho never writes into the app; the app may pull from Zoho when it opens something (H07 pulls the Desk thread).
  • Writes into the app by staff (edits to client data, flags, deletions, publishing) stay in the app; that is the whole reason a built surface exists.
  • Bands only in Zoho: no PAN, no financial numbers beyond bands; the record of truth for regulated records is the app's database.
  • The state is computed in the app and mirrored to the Contact; Zoho never computes a state.
  • A level is picked for launch; a higher level is later or never, and the reason says which.

Is the sheet the universal set?

No. The sheet is the generic admin universe of a consumer fintech app, written before the product model existed (the sheet is dated 9 Jul 2026, the v0.1 admin spec 8 Sep 2026). It carries nothing about the client's plan or financial record, the engine, data-quality work, DIFM onboarding, compliance records, AA renewals or deletion requests. Kajal's list of 8 Sep 2026 (the 48 placement rows on the Admin and CRM tab) covers that half. The sheet and that list together are the admin universe; the CRM set (calls, bookings, the pre-call view, nudges, tickets) sits beside it and is left for later here.

  • The client record and financial data: one person's record with source and precision badges, edits on behalf, the engine re-run (own row 1).
  • Data-quality and ops work: unknown ISINs, implausible values, AA fetch failures with manual fallback, refund and reassignment requests, deletion requests (own row 2).
  • Assisted onboarding and DIFM provisioning (own row 3).
  • AA consents and CAS: expiry, renewals (S15), FIP failures, CAS uploads (own row 4).
  • Plan versions and the engine's master data: the logic panel L00 to L09 (own row 5).
  • The nine compliance records, their exports and retention; deletion under DPDP (own row 6).
  • Payments and refunds beyond a subscription list: a la carte receipts, GST, the refund flag (own row 7).
  • The returning-user states board with ladder days (own row 8).
  • The pre-call view, which borders the CRM set (own row 9).
  • The database KPIs (AUA, SIP book, AA availed, plans built, failure rates) are under the sheet's Dashboard row.

The CRM set, later Calls and bookings (K01, K04, Zoho Bookings), the nudge matrix (Zoho Campaigns), tickets and the grievance register (Zoho Desk), call centre tasks (brief V2), DIFM prospects, consent and DND handling: the CRM set, on the Admin and CRM tab, not picked here.

2. OnboardingV2, Zoho
Queues are CRM views by journey stage; resends are Campaigns journeys and call tasks; funnels are Mixpanel.

Onboarding in yeslyf is not a four-stage funnel (registration, KYC, AA consent, profile setup) but the returning-user states S1 to S5, with KYC at P02 and AA at A06 to A09. The state is computed in the app and mirrored to the Contact as the journey stage; KYC and AA statuses are mirrored fields. So 'pending onboarding, incomplete registration, failed KYC, pending AA consent' are four saved views in the CRM, made by hand like the eleven in the trial.

Progress by stage and drop-off points (the enhanced views) are Mixpanel funnels: reveal (R01 to R09), paywall (R12 to P05), data (O02 to D10), with the time between first and last step. Counts per state are an Analytics tile over the journey stage.

The 'resend' actions are the nudge matrix: Campaigns journeys keyed on the journey stage send the reminders on the ladder (S1a to S5a), and a human call is a CRM task (brief V2). A KYC or AA request is not resent by an admin; the nudge sends the person back to P02 or A06 in the app. A one-off send is a Campaigns send. Nothing is built.

The analytics (funnel performance, abandonment, conversion) is Mixpanel. An onboarding SLA is not defined; nothing to measure yet.
