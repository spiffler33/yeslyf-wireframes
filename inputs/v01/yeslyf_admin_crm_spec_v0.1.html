Admin, CRM and nudges: how they connect to the wireframes

Companion to the journey wireframes v0.1. Every item in the House of Alpha admin-panel document is placed into one of four buckets, the Zoho connection is specified down to fields and events, and the nudge matrix is here for Bhuvanaa to write into directly. Verdicts and comments save in this browser; Export produces the change list.

The app holds the money; Zoho holds the relationship. The app is the hospital record; Zoho is the front desk and the phone. Zoho never sees the diagnosis.
  • Four buckets: bought consoles (zero dev), low-code tools over the database (days of configuration), the logic panel (built), and items that fold into something else or change because they conflict with a decision already taken.
  • Screen IDs (A02, R09, N01) are the wireframe screens. If this file and the wireframe file sit in the same folder, the IDs are links.
  • States S0 to S15 are the returning-user table (N01). They are the Zoho journey-stage picklist, verbatim.
  • Nothing here changes a decision from the wireframe review; where the admin doc conflicts with one, the row says so.

Events: app to Zoho

The devs build one emitter. Zoho Flow maps each event to Contact fields and a timeline note. State is computed in the app and mirrored; Zoho never computes state.

Channel rules

One master message per nudge; the channel policy is a column, changed without touching the app. P = push, W = WhatsApp, E = email, S = SMS, B = in-app banner.

  • Service and money (OTP, receipts, mandates, agreement, plan ready, call reminders, payment failed): WhatsApp utility template plus push; email when there is a document.
  • Behaviour nudges (not paid, data stalled, plan unread, no action, update not accepted): push first if the app is installed and permission granted; WhatsApp second; email as the long-form fallback.
  • Documents (plan PDF, invoices, agreement copies, renewals): email always; WhatsApp pointer optional.
  • State changes on open (plan updated, review due, payment failed): in-app banner on H01; no send if they open anyway.
  • WhatsApp needs opt-in (captured with the OTP at A02) and Meta-approved templates; free text only within 24 hours of the user messaging first. Marketing templates cost several times utility templates; verify current pricing before choosing policies.
  • SMS only for OTP and payment alerts; DND blocks promotional SMS.
  • Push permission is asked right after P05, not at first open.
  • One nudge per person per week across channels, enforced in Zoho. Service messages are exempt.
